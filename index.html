Regulation (EU) 2023/1115

Built for EUDR due diligence.

The EU Deforestation Regulation bars seven commodities and their derived products from the EU market, or from export, unless they are deforestation-free and legally produced. Proving that is a traceability problem.

What an operator has to show

An operator placing a relevant product on the EU market for the first time carries out due diligence and submits a due diligence statement to the EUDR Information System, a tool on the EU's TRACES platform. Per shipment that means:

  • geolocation of the plots of land where the commodity was produced
  • chain-of-custody records linking those plots to the shipment
  • evidence of legal production in the country of origin
  • a risk assessment, and mitigation where risk is not negligible

Covered commodities: cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soya and wood, plus the derived products listed in Annex I. Of the materials above, coffee is in scope.
